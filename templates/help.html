📝 This app contains a few thoughtful questions where on answering them, you will get to know about your mental being and current emotional state. 💚
The 1st page after clicking "Proceed" takes you to the Normal Lifestyle Questionnaire where you'll answer 9 basic lifestyle questions about sleep, work, exercise, and more. 🌙
The 2nd page is the GAD-7 — Anxiety Score Calculator You'll answer 7 questions and your anxiety score will be determined based on your responses. This is a clinically validated tool!
The 3rd page is the PHQ-9 — Depression Score Measurement 💙 You'll answer 9 questions and the depression score will be finalized. Also a clinically validated questionnaire!
The Result Page will give you your personalized mental health summary along with unique solutions tailored to your condition and a downloadable PDF report!
All questions are compulsory to attempt — no skipping allowed! Fill them carefully as your answers shape your result. 🔒
💭 Choose the answer that best describes how you feel right now. Be honest — this helps the system truly understand your emotional wellbeing. 🌱
🔒 Be honest — your answers are completely private. Nobody else can see them. The more honest you are, the better insights you'll receive! 💚
After completing all questions, you'll receive a full result summary and you can download your mental health report as a PDF — no printer needed!
Fill the questions cautiously as they are immutable/non-changeable once you move to the next question. Take your time!
